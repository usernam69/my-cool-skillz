# Make It Better

## Overview
Enhances an app or project by making one bold change that improves conversions or look and feel.

## Steps
1. **Look at the site first.** Open and review the current app/project (pages, UI, flows) before changing anything.
2. **Think about the user experience.** Consider who the user is, what they want, and where friction or drop-off happens. Think outside the box.
3. **Make one bold change.** Pick a single high-impact change — not a small tweak. It should noticeably improve conversions or the look and feel. Use any tool available.
4. **Ship it.** Implement, test, and commit the change. You must ship a change every time.

## Common Mistakes
- Making small, incremental tweaks instead of one bold change.
- Changing things without first looking at the site.
- Over-engineering or shipping nothing.
